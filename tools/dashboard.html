1Why a foveated map?

A robot needs fine detail close by, where it can collide, and only a coarse picture far away. AdaptMap keeps 5 cm cells near the robot and coarser ones further out, like the human eye's fovea.

2What it sees

Live top-down map around the robot (white). Each zone is drawn at its own cell size.
